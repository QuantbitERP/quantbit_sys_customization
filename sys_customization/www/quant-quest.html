🧾 Quant-Quest 2025 – Rules & Guidelines

Organized by Quantbit Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Objective

Quant-Quest 2025 is a recruitment hackathon designed to identify passionate, creative, and skilled developers capable of solving real-world problems using technology. Participants will choose one problem statement from the given list and build a working prototype.

Team Formation

  • Participants can compete individually or in teams of up to 5 members.
  • Team members must register before the hackathon starts.
  • Each team must select only one problem statement and stick to it throughout the event.

Technology Stack

Participants can use any of the following tech stacks:

  • Backend: Python (Flask, Django, Frappe), Node.js
  • Frontend: React, Vue, Angular, or any modern JS framework
  • Mobile: Flutter, React Native
  • Database: MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, or Firebase
  • AI/ML Tools (optional): OpenAI API, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn

⚠️ Internet access is allowed. However, use of pre-built templates or previously completed projects will be disqualified.

Deliverables

  • Source code (GitHub / GitLab link).
  • Short presentation (3–5 slides) explaining problem, solution, and tech stack.
  • Working demo (live link or recorded video < 3 min).
  • Submission deadline: 4 PM on event day.

Development Rules

  • Projects must be original and developed by the registered team.
  • Use of open-source components is allowed, but copying pre-built solutions is not.
  • Teams must maintain a public repository (GitHub/GitLab) with commits.
  • Include a README file explaining setup, purpose, and features.
  • Judges may review commits to ensure fairness.
  • Plagiarism or rule violations result in disqualification.

Basic Rules

  • Event Duration: 1-Day Hackathon (9 AM – 6 PM).
  • Problem Statement Release: 24 hours before the event. Participants may plan and start working early.
  • Each team must develop a working prototype and present it by the end of the event.
  • Any programming language or framework may be used (Python, Full Stack, Flutter recommended).
  • Internet access is permitted for documentation, libraries, and APIs.
  • Maintain professional and respectful conduct.
  • Organizers may make rule modifications if required.
  • All updates and announcements will be shared through the official Hackathon telegram group.
  • Participants are responsible for staying informed and meeting deadlines.

Rewards & Opportunities

  • Internship Opportunities with attractive stipend at Quantbit Technologies Pvt. Ltd.
  • Certificates of Excellence and Participation.
  • Job Offer Opportunities (3 LPA +).
